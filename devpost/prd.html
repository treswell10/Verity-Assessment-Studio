Verity Assessment Studio

AI proposes marks question by question. The teacher confirms, corrects, and signs off — nothing becomes final without them.

The Core Journey

Step through the eight stages. Each one is a screen the teacher actually sees.

Look and Feel

Clean, modern, professional, but approachable — calm and trustworthy. Large cards, clear buttons, minimal text. No complex dashboards.

White / light background
Navy — primary
Green — confirmed/correct
Amber — teacher review needed
Red — errors only

States & Boundaries

NORMAL Marking

AI proposes with sufficient confidence; teacher confirms or changes.

ESCALATED Teacher Review Needed

Low confidence; blocks advancing until the teacher confirms or changes the mark.

BLOCKED Sign-off with unresolved review

"⚠️ 1 question still needs your review." Sign-off stays unavailable until resolved.

ERROR Unreadable upload

"We couldn't read this file. Please upload a clear PDF or image and try again."

ERROR Blank file

"No content was found in this file." → Upload Another File.

FINAL Mark Finalised

Locked, labeled reviewed and approved by the educator.

Acceptance Criteria (checkable during build)

Question-by-question marking
  • ☐ Each question shows proposed mark, reasoning, and confidence before the teacher acts.
  • ☐ Confirming a question advances to the next one automatically.
Escalation on low confidence
  • ☐ A low-confidence question is visibly flagged "Teacher Review Needed," not silently proposed.
  • ☐ Marking cannot advance past a flagged question until resolved.
Sign-off is the only path to final
  • ☐ Sign-off is unavailable while any question is unresolved.
  • ☐ After sign-off, the screen clearly states the mark is final and reviewed by the educator.

What We're Building vs. Deferred

Building now
  • One memo + one learner script, full click-through to sign-off
  • Visible AI reasoning and confidence per question
  • Teacher override at every question, same controls for normal/escalated
  • Hard block on sign-off while unresolved
  • Graceful handling of unreadable/blank uploads
Deferred from the PoC
  • Bulk/batch script processing, ADF scanner, QR separators
  • Handwriting OCR
  • Mark-sheet export, class totals, question-level analytics
  • Authentication, multi-teacher, role-based access
Open questions
