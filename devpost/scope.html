Verity

AI-Assisted Marking with the Educator in the Loop — a proof of concept that gives teaching time back without taking the final decision away from the teacher.

The Unique Kernel

AI proposes a mark and explains its reasoning for every question. When it's uncertain, it escalates to the teacher instead of guessing. The teacher reviews, confirms or overrides, and signs off. No mark reaches a learner on the software's authority alone.

Who & Why

Who it's for

An ordinary South African public school teacher — picture a Mathematics teacher in Gauteng, Limpopo, Mpumalanga, the Eastern Cape, or KwaZulu-Natal — with a school laptop and a stack of scripts to mark after a long teaching day. Today, every script is marked from scratch, by hand.

Why it matters

These are children's school scripts, not anonymous documents. Professional judgment stays decisive wherever context matters — and the educator remains accountable for every final mark.

What "Working" Looks Like

The "oh, that's cool" beat: Demo in under a minute — upload a memo, upload a script, watch Verity mark Q1.1 and Q1.2 with ticks, marks, and reasons, then recognize a valid alternative method or a carried-forward error and propose partial credit. Then it hits an ambiguous answer and flags it for the teacher instead of guessing. The teacher reviews, signs off, and the mark locks in.

Scope Boundary

In this PoC NOW
  • Memo upload with question numbers, expected answers, mark allocations
  • One learner script, pre-scanned or typed — clear, readable answers (no handwriting OCR)
  • Question-by-question marking: proposed mark + short reasoning
  • Confidence-based escalation: ambiguous answers flagged, not auto-marked
  • Teacher review: confirm, override, or award partial/half marks
  • Sign-off step that finalizes the mark
Worth doing later LATER
  • Live ADF scanner integration, QR separator sheets, bulk batch processing of a full class
  • Handwriting OCR
  • Mark totals/percentages, mark-sheet export, question-level analytics for intervention
  • Dashboards, role-based access, audit trails, on-prem deployment, data retention rules
Explicitly cut CUT
  • Multi-teacher / department workflows — single teacher, single script for this PoC
  • Report generation and NSC achievement-level export — belongs to the later analytics story
  • Any real infrastructure/deployment concerns — a hackathon PoC runs locally
